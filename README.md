IEEE is a globally recognized professional organization dedicated to advancing 
technology and fostering innovation across various engineering disciplines.

This code was developed by the current IEEE Electrical Engineering (ELG) Commissioner 
at the University of Ottawa student branch as an initiative to provide students with 
opportunities to strengthen their technical skills, gain hands-on experience, and 
become more proficient in working with microcontrollers and embedded systems. Through 
this project, students are encouraged to develop their problem-solving abilities, 
expand their practical knowledge, and build confidence in applying theoretical 
concepts to real-world engineering applications.
